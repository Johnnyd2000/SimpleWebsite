GOOD QUESTIONS. SWIFT ANSWERS.

Frequently asked questions

Can you deliver my homework before class?

We can move a package, but we cannot negotiate your deadline! Our pretend campus delivery service handles supplies and parcels, not homework miracles.

Do you ship internationally?

In our fictional business, yes! Our global freight team serves more than 120 countries and helps customers plan their customs paperwork.

Can I ship a birthday cake?

In our pretend store, we would recommend protective, temperature-appropriate packaging and a suitable delivery service. Birthday candles ride unlit, of course.

Where is my package?

This class website has no live tracking. In our fictional service, you would use the tracking number from your shipping receipt or contact our support team.

Will this form book a real delivery?

No. SwiftHaul is a fictional class project. The form only displays a practice confirmation and does not send a message or create a shipment.
